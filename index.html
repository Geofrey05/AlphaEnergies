Farm Success
March 2026 Nakuru

From Dry Seasons to Year-Round Harvest

James Otieno Farm Owner, 12 acres

For years, James Otieno struggled with unreliable rainfall and expensive diesel pumps on his 12-acre mixed farm in Nakuru. “Some seasons we lost almost half the crop…”

For years, James Otieno struggled with unreliable rainfall and expensive diesel pumps on his 12-acre mixed farm in Nakuru. “Some seasons we lost almost half the crop because we couldn’t water consistently,” he recalls. “Diesel costs kept rising and the generator was always breaking down.”

In late 2025, James contacted Alpha-Energy after a neighbour recommended us. We carried out a free site survey and designed a complete package: a 90-metre borehole, a high-efficiency solar submersible pump, 3.5 kW of solar panels, and a drip irrigation network covering his vegetable and maize plots.

The system was installed in under two weeks under our hire-purchase plan. “I didn’t have to pay everything upfront. That made a huge difference,” James says. Within the first season he expanded his vegetable production and started supplying two local markets regularly.

“My water bill is now zero, the pump runs silently every sunny day, and I sleep better knowing the crops are covered. Alpha-Energy didn’t just sell me equipment — they gave me a solution that works.”

Home Energy
January 2026 Kiambu

“No More Blackouts” — Reliable Power at Home

Grace Wanjiku Homeowner & Shop Owner

Grace Wanjiku runs a small tailoring shop from her home in Kiambu. Frequent power outages meant lost working hours and high electricity bills…

Grace Wanjiku runs a small tailoring and shop from her home in Kiambu. Frequent power outages meant lost working hours, damaged appliances and high electricity bills. “Some weeks the lights would go off three or four times. My sewing machines would stop mid-order and customers would get frustrated,” she explains.

She reached out to Alpha-Energy looking for a home solar solution with battery backup. After assessing her daily load (lighting, fridge, sewing machines, TV and charging), we installed a 5 kW hybrid solar system with 10 kWh lithium battery storage and a clean inverter.

The installation was completed in three days. “From the moment they switched it on, the house felt different,” Grace says. “Even when the grid goes off, everything keeps running. My shop no longer loses hours, and my monthly electricity bill dropped by more than 60%.”

Grace chose the hire-purchase option so she could start benefiting immediately while spreading payments over manageable instalments. “Alpha-Energy explained everything clearly. The team was professional, the system is quiet, and the monitoring app shows me exactly how much power I’m producing and saving. I wish I had done this earlier.”
